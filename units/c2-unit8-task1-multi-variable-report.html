Unit 8: Complex Multi-Variable Report

Skill focus: Task 1 — Multi-Variable Synthesis (Complex)

1 Mini Lesson

With four or more categories moving in different, irregular directions, a single comparison word repeated four times will flatten your Lexical Resource score. At this level, match precise language to the size of each change — distinguish a figure that "more than doubled" from one that shifted only "marginally", and one that fell "considerably" from one that declined only slightly.

Among the four categories, ___ saw the most dramatic increase, ___ (precise language). By contrast, ___ declined ___ (precise language), while ___ ___ (marginal change) and ___ ___ (moderate change). Taken together, ___ (synthesis).

Precision words like "considerably," "marginally," "moderately," and "substantially" let you show a genuine range of movement without repeating the same comparative phrase.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (household energy sources) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • Among the four household energy sources tracked, solar power usage saw the most dramatic increase, rising from a mere 4% to 35% of households as costs fell sharply. By contrast, coal usage declined considerably, dropping from 38% to 12%, while gas usage held broadly steady, edging down only marginally from 40% to 36%, and electricity-only heating dropped moderately from 18% to 17%. Taken together, the data reveals a decisive structural shift away from fossil fuels toward solar generation.

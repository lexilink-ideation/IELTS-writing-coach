C2 Task 2

Unit 7: Dialectical Synthesis Essay

Skill focus: Task 2 — Thesis-Antithesis-Synthesis

1 Mini Lesson

A true dialectical structure goes beyond claim-and-counter-perspective. State a genuine thesis, present a competing antithesis built on a different underlying framework, then produce a synthesis that transcends both rather than simply averaging them.

The thesis that ___ (initial position) holds considerable merit. The antithesis emerges when we consider that ___ (competing framework). A genuine synthesis recognises that ___ (resolution that transcends both), rather than simply splitting the difference between the two positions.

A weak synthesis just says "both sides have a point." A strong one reframes the question so the original opposition dissolves.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (AI tools and homework) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • The thesis that AI tools undermine independent learning by supplying instant answers holds considerable merit, particularly where students substitute genuine understanding for copied output. The antithesis emerges when we consider that traditional homework itself was never a pure measure of understanding, often rewarding rote repetition over genuine insight. A genuine synthesis recognises that the real pedagogical challenge lies not in banning AI but in redesigning homework so that it demands the kind of reasoning AI cannot simply replicate, rather than treating the technology itself as the enemy.

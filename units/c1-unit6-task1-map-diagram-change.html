Unit 6: Map/Diagram Change Description

Skill focus: Task 1 — Change Over Time (Map)

1 Mini Lesson

Map or diagram tasks reward precise directional language combined with clear change verbs. Locate each feature relative to a fixed landmark, then say exactly what happened to it — constructed, demolished, replaced, or expanded.

To the ___ (direction) of ___ (landmark), a new ___ (feature) was constructed. The former ___ (feature) was demolished and replaced by ___ (new feature). ___ (feature) was significantly expanded to include ___.

Anchoring each change to a direction and a fixed landmark gives the reader a clear mental map, rather than a loose list of changes.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (a town centre before and after redevelopment) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new map below.

  • To the north of the old town hall, a new shopping centre was constructed, replacing what had previously been an open car park. The original market square was significantly expanded to accommodate a public garden, while the row of small shops to the south was demolished and replaced by a modern library.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the changes in the table below (not the model paragraph above):

To the north of the main entrance, ___ (change 1). To the south, ___ (change 2). At the centre of the campus, ___ (change 3).

Now write your full description yourself in the writing box below.

A2 Task 2

Unit 9: Two Advantages, No Opinion

Skill focus: Task 2 — Advantages List (Simple)

1 Mini Lesson

You can list two advantages of something without giving your own opinion. Use "firstly" and "secondly" to introduce each advantage — do not use "I think" or "in my opinion" anywhere in this task.

Firstly, one advantage of ___ is that ___. Secondly, another advantage is that ___.

This is a zero-opinion task: your job is only to name two clear advantages, not to say what you personally believe.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (online classes) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • Firstly, one advantage of online classes is that students can study at home. Secondly, another advantage is that classes are often cheaper than in-person courses.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model sentences above):

Firstly, one advantage of AI tools for homework is that ___. Secondly, another advantage is that ___.

Now write your full answer yourself in the writing box below — remember, no personal opinion.

4 Writing Box

Prompt: What are the advantages of students using AI tools to help with their homework? Give two advantages.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My first sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "Firstly" for my first advantage.
  • I used "Secondly" for my second advantage.
  • I did NOT use "I think" or "in my opinion" — this task has no personal opinion.
  • My answer is about AI and homework.

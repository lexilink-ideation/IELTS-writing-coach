Unit 8: Table and Chart Synthesis

Skill focus: Task 1 — Two-Format Data Synthesis

1 Mini Lesson

Sometimes a Task 1 question does not give you two matching charts — it gives you a table (a fixed snapshot) alongside a trend described in words. Your job is to weave the two into one coherent paragraph, not report them as separate, disconnected pieces.

While ___ (top category from the table) was the most popular choice in ___ (year), accounting for ___% of the total, this followed a period of ___ (change language) — with the wider figure rising from ___% in ___ to ___% in ___. Taken together, ___ (synthesis).

A synthesis sentence that explicitly links the static table to the described trend shows the examiner you understood the relationship between the two data sources.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (recycling rates by material) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • While paper was the most commonly recycled material in 2022, accounting for 42% of all recycled waste ahead of glass at 30% and plastic at 18%, this snapshot followed a period of steady growth in overall household recycling. Council figures show that the proportion of households recycling regularly rose from 35% in 2014 to 68% in 2022, with the sharpest increase occurring after 2018. Taken together, the data suggests that as recycling became more widespread among households, paper emerged as the material collected in the greatest volume.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the table and the described trend below (not the model paragraph above):

While ___ (top destination) was the most popular choice for studying abroad in 2023, accounting for ___% of applicants, this followed a period of ___ (change language) — with overall interest rising from ___% in 2015 to ___% in 2023. Taken together, ___ (synthesis).

Now write your full synthesized paragraph yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I reported Australia's table figure (38%) as the top destination.
  • I reported the described trend's start and end figures (22% in 2015 and 50% in 2023).
  • I used a connector such as "while" or "meanwhile" to link the table and the trend.
  • I closed with a synthesis phrase such as "taken together" or "overall".
  • My paragraph is about studying abroad.

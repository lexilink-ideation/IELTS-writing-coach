B2 Task 1

Unit 12: Table Report: Grouping Small Numbers

Skill focus: Task 1 — Table Selectivity

1 Mini Lesson

When a table has several small percentages, do not list each one separately — group them together with a phrase like "each account for around ___% or less". Use "by contrast" to mark the shift from the standout figure to the grouped minor figures.

The table shows ___ (topic). Overall, ___ (standout reason) stands out as the main reason, cited by ___% of respondents, considerably higher than any other reason. By contrast, ___, ___, and ___ each account for around ___% or less, figures close enough to group together.

Grouping small figures and singling out the standout figure together form the real table-selectivity strategy examiners reward.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (reasons customers choose online shopping) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • The table shows the reasons given by customers for choosing online shopping. Overall, convenience stands out as the main reason, cited by 66% of customers, considerably higher than any other factor. By contrast, lower prices, wider selection, and product reviews each account for around 10% or less, figures close enough to group together rather than describe individually.

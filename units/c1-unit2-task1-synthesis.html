C1 Task 1

Unit 2: Multi-Chart Comparison

Skill focus: Task 1 — Data Synthesis

1 Mini Lesson

When a question gives you two charts, do not describe them one after another as separate paragraphs. Instead, synthesize them: report what each shows at its starting point, how the picture changed, and a closing statement that links both charts together.

In ___ (first year), ___ (relationship between categories). By ___ (second year), ___ (how this changed). Overall, ___ (statement linking both charts).

A synthesis sentence at the end shows the examiner you understood the relationship between the two data sets, not just the individual numbers.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (public transport modes) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • In 2015, buses were the dominant mode of public transport, accounting for half of all journeys, compared to just 30% for trains. By 2023, this pattern had reversed, with train usage rising to 55% while bus usage fell to 35%. Overall, the data indicates a clear shift in commuter preference from road-based to rail-based transport over the eight-year period.

3 Guided Practice

In 2020, ___ (undergraduate vs graduate enrolment). By 2024, ___ (how this changed). Overall, ___ (statement linking both charts).

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I reported the 2020 figures (40% and 55%).
  • I reported the 2024 figures (65% and 50%).
  • I used a contrast connector such as "whereas" or "while" to link the two years.
  • I closed with a synthesis phrase such as "overall" or "taken together".
  • I mentioned "undergraduate" and "graduate".
  • I wrote at least 55 words.

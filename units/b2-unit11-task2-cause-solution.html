B2 Task 2

Unit 11: Cause, Then Solution

Skill focus: Task 2 — Cause-Solution Essay

1 Mini Lesson

A Cause-Solution essay asks why a trend is happening, then what should be done about it. This is different from a Problem-Solution essay, which asks what problems a trend creates. Here, keep your causes and your solutions in two clearly separate sections — do not blend them together.

One key cause of ___ (trend) is ___ (cause 1). Another cause is ___ (cause 2). To address this, ___ (solution) could help, since ___ (result).

Two clear causes, followed by a solution linked to a result, is the strongest way to fully develop this essay type.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (students relying on AI tools for homework) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • One key cause of students' growing reliance on AI tools for homework is that these tools provide instant answers without any of the friction of independent research. Another cause is the increasing academic pressure many students face, which pushes them toward the fastest available solution. To address this, schools could redesign assignments to require personal reflection that AI cannot easily replicate, since this would encourage genuine engagement with the material.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

One key cause of the growing movement from the countryside to cities is ___ (cause 1). Another cause is ___ (cause 2). To address this, ___ (solution) could help, since ___ (result).

Now write your full paragraph yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I stated a first cause using "one key cause is" or similar.
  • I stated a second cause using "another cause is".
  • I proposed a solution using "to address this" or "one solution would be".
  • I linked my solution to a result using "since" or "this would".
  • My causes and my solution are clearly separate, not blended together.
  • My paragraph is about cities and the countryside.
  • I wrote at least 40 words.

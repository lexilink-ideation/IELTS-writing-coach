C1 Task 1

Unit 4: Academic Process Diagram

Skill focus: Task 1 — Process Description (Academic)

1 Mini Lesson

Academic or institutional process diagrams reward clear stage-sequencing language and appropriate use of the passive voice — since the process matters more than who performs each action. Move through every stage in order, without skipping any.

The process begins with ___ (stage 1). Subsequently, ___ (stage 2, passive voice: is/are + past participle). This is followed by ___ (stage 3). At the penultimate stage, ___ (stage 4). The process culminates in ___ (final stage).

The passive voice ("the manuscript is reviewed") keeps the focus on the process itself, which is standard in academic report writing.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (academic journal peer review) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new process below.

  • The process begins with a researcher submitting a manuscript for consideration. Subsequently, the manuscript is reviewed by independent experts in the field. This is followed by a revision stage, during which feedback is incorporated by the author. At the penultimate stage, the revised manuscript is re-evaluated by the editorial board. The process culminates in the final approval and publication of the article.

4 Writing Box

Stages: Developing an Online Course

StageWhat Happens

2. Curriculum DesignMap learning objectives and sequence the content
3. Content ProductionRecord video lectures and create supporting materials
4. PilotingTest the course with a small group and collect feedback
5. Full LaunchRelease the finished course to all enrolled students

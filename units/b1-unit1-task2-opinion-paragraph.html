B1 Task 2

Unit 1: Full Opinion Paragraph

Skill focus: Task 2 — Opinion + Two Reasons + Example

1 Mini Lesson

A full IELTS Task 2 paragraph at this level needs more than one reason. Use sequencing words to organise two reasons clearly, then close with a concrete example.

Opinion. Firstly, reason one. Secondly, reason two because ___. For example, ___.

"Firstly" and "secondly" help the examiner follow your structure. "For example" shows you can support an idea with a specific detail, not just a general claim.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (studying abroad) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • In my opinion, studying abroad is beneficial for students. Firstly, it helps them learn about different cultures. Secondly, it improves their independence because they must solve problems on their own. For example, many students learn to manage money and schedules without their parents' help.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

___ (In my opinion / I believe) automation is / is not good for workers. Firstly, ___. Secondly, ___ because ___. For example, ___.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My paragraph starts with a clear opinion.
  • I used "Firstly" and/or "Secondly" to sequence my reasons.
  • I used "because" to explain at least one reason.
  • I used "For example" to give a concrete example.
  • My paragraph is about automation and jobs.
  • I wrote at least 35 words.

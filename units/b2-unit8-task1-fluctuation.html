B2 Task 1

Unit 8: Line Graph Fluctuation

Skill focus: Task 1 — Fluctuation Description

1 Mini Lesson

Not every line graph moves in one clear direction. When a trend rises and falls several times, use "fluctuated", "peaked at", and "levelled off" to describe the movement precisely. Whenever you read a figure from a graph rather than a stated exact number, add "about" or "approximately" to show it is an estimate.

___ (category) fluctuated ___ (time period), peaking at approximately ___% in ___ (year), before levelling off at around ___% by ___ (year).

Naming the peak and the point where the trend stabilises shows the examiner you can read irregular data accurately, not just describe a single straight line.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (the price of coffee) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • The price of coffee fluctuated considerably between 2015 and 2020. It rose sharply to a peak of approximately $6 per kilogram in 2017, before falling back and eventually levelling off at around $4 by 2020.

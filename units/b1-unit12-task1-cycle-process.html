B1 Task 1

Unit 12: A Cycle in Order

Skill focus: Task 1 — Process (Cycle Type)

1 Mini Lesson

A process diagram can show a natural cycle — a sequence with no fixed start or end that keeps repeating, unlike a man-made process, which moves toward one final product. When describing a cycle, pick any stage to begin with, sequence the other stages, then show the sequence returns to the first stage and repeats.

The cycle begins with ___ (stage 1). Then, ___ (stage 2). After that, ___ (stage 3). Finally, ___ (stage 4), before the cycle returns to stage 1 and begins again.

Showing the return to stage 1 is what proves to the examiner you understood this is a cycle, not a one-way process.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (the life cycle of a frog) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new cycle below.

  • The cycle begins with a frog laying eggs in water. Then, the eggs hatch into tadpoles. After that, the tadpoles grow legs and develop into young frogs. Finally, the frogs mature and return to the water to lay their own eggs, and the cycle begins again.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the stages in the table below (not the model paragraph above):

The cycle begins with ___ (evaporation). Then, ___ (condensation). After that, ___ (precipitation). Finally, ___ (collection), before the cycle returns to stage 1 and begins again.

Now write your full description yourself in the writing box below.

4 Writing Box

Prompt: The diagram below shows the water cycle in nature. Describe the cycle in order, and show that it repeats — this is a CYCLE, not a one-way process with a fixed end point.

Stages: The Water Cycle

StageWhat Happens
1. EvaporationHeat from the sun causes water in oceans and rivers to evaporate into the air
2. CondensationThe water vapour rises and cools, forming clouds
3. PrecipitationWater falls back to earth as rain or snow
4. CollectionThe water collects in rivers, lakes, and oceans, ready to evaporate again

Look at the stages. Now describe the whole cycle in order, and show it repeats — don't copy the lesson example.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My first sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I opened with "the cycle begins with".
  • I used "then" and "after that" to sequence the middle stages.
  • I closed by showing the cycle returns to stage 1 and repeats.
  • My description mentions evaporation or the water cycle.
  • I wrote at least 32 words.

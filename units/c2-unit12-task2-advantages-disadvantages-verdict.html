C2 Task 2

Unit 12: Advantages, Disadvantages, and a Verdict

Skill focus: Task 2 — Full Weighing Essay

1 Mini Lesson

This unit synthesises the two Advantages & Disadvantages essay subtypes into one complete response. First, present a genuinely balanced range of advantages and disadvantages, as the no-opinion Type 1 essay requires. Then, explicitly weigh the two sides against each other using "outweigh" language, as Type 2 requires. Finally, close with a verdict that is clearly justified — not just stated — and hedged where appropriate, since few real-world questions have an absolute answer.

While ___ (topic) offers benefits such as ___ and ___, it also brings drawbacks such as ___ and ___. Weighing these points, the ___ arguably outweigh the ___, since ___ (justification). On balance, therefore, ___ (verdict), provided ___ (qualifier).

A strong C2 verdict is rarely absolute — it is usually qualified with a condition, which shows the examiner you understand the limits of your own argument.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (automation at work) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • While automating routine workplace tasks offers benefits such as increased efficiency and reduced human error, it also brings drawbacks such as job losses in certain sectors and a growing skills gap among displaced workers. Weighing these points, the long-term economic benefits arguably outweigh the disruption, since automation ultimately creates new categories of skilled work even as it eliminates old ones. On balance, therefore, embracing automation is preferable to resisting it, provided governments invest seriously in retraining programmes for affected workers.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I named at least two advantages of city life.
  • I named at least two disadvantages of city life.
  • I used "outweigh" language to explicitly weigh the two sides.
  • I closed with a verdict using "on balance" or a similar phrase.
  • I justified my verdict with "since" or "because" rather than just stating it.
  • My response is about cities and the countryside.

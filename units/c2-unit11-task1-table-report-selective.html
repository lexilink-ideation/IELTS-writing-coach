Unit 11: Full Table Report: Selectivity Under Pressure

Skill focus: Task 1 — Table Report (Full, Selective)

1 Mini Lesson

A complete table report at this level needs two disciplines working together. First, selectivity: single out the standout figure and group the smaller figures together rather than listing every number in turn. Second, avoiding risky paraphrasing: reach for vocabulary you are fully confident is accurate — like "percentage" and "account for" — instead of inventing an unfamiliar synonym that might introduce an error. A correct simple word always beats an incorrect impressive one.

The table shows ___ (topic). Overall, ___ (standout category) stands out at ___%, considerably higher than ___ (second category) at ___%, while ___ (grouped small categories) each account for under ___%. Taken together, ___ (synthesis).

Notice how "account for" and "percentage" are reused deliberately — they are safe, precise, and always correct.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (destination countries chosen by international students) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • The table shows the destination countries chosen by international students in a given year. Overall, the United States stands out as the most popular destination at 45%, considerably higher than Canada, the second most popular choice at 32%. By contrast, the United Kingdom, Australia, and other destinations each account for under 10% of students, figures close enough to group together rather than describe individually. Taken together, the data indicates that the two largest English-speaking markets attract the overwhelming majority of international students, leaving all other destinations as minor alternatives.

The table shows ___ (topic). Overall, the bus stands out at ___%, considerably higher than the car, the second most common choice at ___%. By contrast, ___ (three modes) each account for under ___%. Taken together, ___ (synthesis).

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I grouped the train, bicycle, and walking together using "account for" and "under 10%".

B1 Task 2

Unit 7: Both Views Plus Opinion

Skill focus: Task 2 — Balanced Viewpoint (Full)

1 Mini Lesson

You already know how to state both views briefly. Now make your own opinion sentence stronger by adding "because" and a real reason — not just a bare statement of your view.

Some people believe ___ (view one). Others believe ___ (view two). In my opinion, ___ (your view), because ___ (reason).

A reason after "because" turns your opinion from a simple statement into a supported argument.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (automation and jobs) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • Some people believe automation will destroy jobs and increase unemployment. Others believe it will create new kinds of jobs that do not exist yet. In my opinion, automation will ultimately create more opportunities than it removes, because history shows that new technologies tend to generate entirely new industries.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

Some people believe ___ (view about studying abroad). Others believe ___ (opposing view). In my opinion, ___, because ___.

Now write your full paragraph yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I used "Others believe" to state the second view.
  • I closed with "In my opinion" and my own view.
  • I supported my opinion with "because" and a real reason.

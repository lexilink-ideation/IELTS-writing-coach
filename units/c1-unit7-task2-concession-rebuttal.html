Unit 7: Counter-Argument with Concession

Skill focus: Task 2 — Concession and Rebuttal

1 Mini Lesson

Move beyond a simple "it is true that... however..." structure. Use the more formal "while it is true that" to concede a point fully, then "it is nonetheless the case that" to rebut it, and close with a broader implication.

While it is true that ___ (opposing view), it is nonetheless the case that ___ (your rebuttal). This suggests that ___ (broader implication).

"Nonetheless" carries more formal weight than "however", and closing with an implication shows you can reason beyond the immediate point.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (city versus countryside living) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • While it is true that countryside living offers cleaner air and a slower pace of life, it is nonetheless the case that access to healthcare, education, and employment remains significantly more limited outside urban centres. This suggests that, for most young professionals, the practical advantages of city life continue to outweigh its drawbacks.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

While it is true that requiring older people to learn digital skills ___ (opposing view), it is nonetheless the case that ___ (your rebuttal). This suggests that ___ (implication).

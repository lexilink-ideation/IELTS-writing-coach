B2 Task 2

Unit 5: Two-Part Question Response

Skill focus: Task 2 — Multi-Part Answering

1 Mini Lesson

Some Task 2 prompts ask two questions in one. You must answer both parts clearly and separately — examiners specifically check that neither part is ignored.

There are several reasons why ___ (answer to part 1). In terms of solutions, ___ (answer to part 2).

Keeping the two parts in separate sentences makes it easy for the examiner to see that you addressed the whole question.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (children and screen time) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • There are several reasons why children spend so much time on screens, including easy access to smartphones and tablets from a very young age. In terms of solutions, parents can set clear daily time limits and encourage alternative activities such as sports or reading.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

There are several reasons why ___ (why older people struggle with technology). In terms of solutions, ___ (what can be done to help them).

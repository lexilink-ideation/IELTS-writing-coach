A1 Task 2

Unit 1: Writing One Opinion Sentence

Skill focus: Task 2 — Opinion Writing

1 Mini Lesson

In IELTS Task 2, you almost always need to give your opinion. The easiest way to start is with one of these phrases:

I think...  |  I believe...  |  In my opinion...

After your opinion, add the topic. A complete opinion sentence needs: a capital letter, an opinion phrase, the topic, and a full stop.

2 Model Sentences

  • I think students should use AI for homework.
  • I believe online classes are useful for students.
  • In my opinion, public transport is convenient for cities.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps to build your own opinion sentence:

___ (I think / I believe / In my opinion) ___ students should use AI for ___ (homework).

Now write the full sentence in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • My sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "I think" / "I believe" / "In my opinion".
  • My sentence answers the question about AI and homework.
  • My sentence has at least 7 words.

Unit 2: Complex Data Synthesis

Skill focus: Task 1 — Multi-Source Synthesis

1 Mini Lesson

With three data sources, repeating "more than" for every comparison becomes monotonous and caps your Lexical Resource score. At this level, vary your comparative language precisely — distinguish a dramatic change from a marginal one, and a decline from a plateau — then close with a sentence that synthesizes all three sources.

___ (place) experienced the most dramatic change, ___ (precise figure language). ___ (place) rose/fell only marginally, ___. ___ (place) actually declined, ___. Taken together, ___ (synthesis statement).

Precision words like "considerably," "marginally," "markedly," and "substantially" let you show range without repeating yourself.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (recycling rates by region) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • Across the three regions, recycling rates followed markedly different trajectories between 2010 and 2022. Northshire experienced the most dramatic rise, with rates more than tripling from a modest 15% to over half of all waste by 2022. Southdale's rate crept up only marginally, edging from 40% to 44%, whereas Midtown's figure actually contracted slightly, slipping from 30% down to 25%. Taken together, the data indicates that recycling initiatives had a far greater impact in some regions than in others, with one area even regressing over the period.

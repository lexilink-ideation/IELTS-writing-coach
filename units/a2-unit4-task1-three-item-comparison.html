A2 Task 1

Unit 4: Comparing Three Items

Skill focus: Task 1 — Multi-item Comparison

1 Mini Lesson

When a table has three items, don't just list them one by one. Use "the highest" and "the lowest" to show which item stands out at each end.

___ (item) has the highest ___ (thing), at ___ (number). ___ (item) has the lowest, at ___ (number). ___ (item) is in the middle, at ___ (number).

"The highest" and "the lowest" let you rank the three items instead of just describing them separately.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (favourite school subjects) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • Mathematics has the highest number of votes, at 45. Art has the lowest, at 10. Science is in the middle, at 25.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the data in the table below (not the model sentence above):

___ (place) has the highest population, at ___ (number). ___ (place) has the lowest, at ___ (number). ___ (place) is in the middle, at ___ (number).

5 Self-Review Checklist

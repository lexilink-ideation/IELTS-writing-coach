Unit 2: Full Bar Chart Overview

Skill focus: Task 1 — Overview + Supporting Detail

1 Mini Lesson

A complete bar chart report needs an overview sentence naming the main pattern, followed by two supporting detail sentences: one for the highest figure, one for the lowest — using a contrast connector to link them.

Overall, ___ (main pattern). ___ (highest item) recorded the highest figure, at ___. By contrast, ___ (lowest item) had the lowest, at ___.

"By contrast" and "whereas" signal that you are directly comparing the two extremes, which examiners reward as coherent comparison.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (recycling rates by region) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • Overall, recycling rates varied considerably between the three regions. In the North, over seventy percent of waste was recycled, the highest figure recorded. By contrast, the South recycled just over forty percent, considerably less than the other two regions.

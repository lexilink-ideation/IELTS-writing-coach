A1 Task 2

Unit 11: One Advantage, No Opinion

Skill focus: Task 2 — Advantage Statement (Simple)

1 Mini Lesson

Not every Task 2 sentence needs "I think". Some essays only ask you to state a fact — zero personal opinion. Practice this bare pattern:

One advantage of ___ (topic) is that ___ (benefit).

No "I think", no "I believe" — just a plain, factual advantage. This is the building block of essays that must stay neutral.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (audiobooks) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • One advantage of audiobooks is that people can listen while doing other things.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gap using the topic below (not the model sentence above):

One advantage of ___ (online classes) is that ___ (benefit).

Now write your full sentence yourself in the writing box below. Remember: no "I think" or "I believe".

4 Writing Box

Prompt: Write ONE sentence stating an advantage of online classes. Do NOT give your personal opinion — just state the fact.

Tip: press Enter to check, or Shift+Enter for a new line.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • My sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "One advantage" or "An advantage".
  • My sentence is about online classes or online learning.
  • I did NOT use "I think" or "I believe" — this sentence has no personal opinion.

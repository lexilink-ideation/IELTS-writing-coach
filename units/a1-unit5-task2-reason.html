A1 Task 2

Unit 5: Opinion with One Reason

Skill focus: Task 2 — Reason Expansion

1 Mini Lesson

You already know how to give a simple opinion. Now you will make it stronger by adding a reason. The easiest way to add a reason is with the word:

because

An opinion sentence with a reason has two parts: your opinion, then "because" + your reason.

2 Model Sentences

These examples use a different topic (online classes) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • I think online classes are useful because students can learn from home.
  • I believe online classes save time because students do not need to travel.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model sentences above):

___ (I think / I believe) children should / should not spend a lot of time on screens because ___.

Now write the full sentence yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • My sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "I think" / "I believe".
  • I used "because" to give a reason.
  • My sentence is about children and screens.

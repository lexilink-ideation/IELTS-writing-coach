A2 Task 2

Unit 7: Two Reasons with an Example

Skill focus: Task 2 — Reason Sequencing + Example

1 Mini Lesson

Now combine two skills you already know: sequencing reasons with "firstly" and "secondly", and supporting your second reason with a specific example using "for example".

Opinion. Firstly, reason one. Secondly, reason two. For example, ___ (specific detail).

Adding "for example" after your second reason makes your strongest point the most detailed one.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (free public transport for students) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • I think public transport should be free for students. Firstly, it saves them money. Secondly, it encourages them to travel independently. For example, many students could travel to part-time jobs without relying on their parents.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model sentences above):

___ (I think / I believe) AI should / should not help students with homework. Firstly, ___. Secondly, ___. For example, ___.

Now write your full answer yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My first sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "I think" / "I believe" / "In my opinion".
  • I used "Firstly" and "Secondly" for my two reasons.
  • I used "For example" to add a specific detail.
  • My answer is about AI and homework.

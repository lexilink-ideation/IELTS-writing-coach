A1 Task 1

Unit 12: One Stage of a Process

Skill focus: Task 1 — Process Basics

1 Mini Lesson

Some Task 1 diagrams show a process with several stages. Before describing a whole process, practice naming just one stage in one simple sentence. Use the word "First," to show it is the very first stage.

First, ___ (who) ___ (what happens at stage 1).

One clear sentence about one stage is the first building block of a full process description.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (making a cup of tea) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new process below.

  • First, you boil water in a kettle.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gap using the steps in the table below (not the model sentence above):

First, the ___ (author) ___ (writes the manuscript).

Now write your own sentence about stage 1 only in the writing box below.

4 Writing Box

Prompt: The table below shows the stages of how a book is made. Write ONE sentence naming only the FIRST stage. Start your sentence with "First,".

Steps: How a Book Is Made

StepWhat Happens
1The author writes the manuscript
2The publisher edits and prints the book
3Bookshops sell the finished book to readers

Look at Step 1 only. Now write one sentence about it — don't copy the lesson example.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • My sentence ends with a full stop.
  • My sentence starts with "First,".
  • I mentioned the author writing the manuscript or book.
  • I only described stage 1 — not stages 2 or 3.

B1 Task 2

Unit 3: Both-Views Discussion (Intro)

Skill focus: Task 2 — Balanced Viewpoint Intro

1 Mini Lesson

"Discuss both views" questions ask you to state both sides briefly before giving your own opinion. Keep each side to one sentence, then close with your view.

Some people believe ___ (view one). Others believe ___ (view two). In my opinion, ___ (your view).

Notice this is different from "advantages and disadvantages" — here you are reporting what other people think, not just listing pros and cons.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (studying abroad) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • Some people believe studying abroad broadens students' horizons and builds independence. Others believe it is too expensive and stressful for young people. In my opinion, the benefits usually outweigh the drawbacks for students who plan carefully.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

Some people believe ___ (view about screen time). Others believe ___ (opposing view). In my opinion, ___.

Now write your full paragraph yourself in the writing box below.

4 Writing Box

Prompt: Some people think children spend too much time on screens, while others think screens help children learn. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I used "Some people believe" to state the first view.
  • I used "Others believe" to state the second view.
  • I closed with "In my opinion" and my own view.
  • My paragraph is about children and screen time.

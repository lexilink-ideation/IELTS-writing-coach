A2 Task 1

Unit 8: Simple Overview Sentence

Skill focus: Task 1 — Overview (Intro)

1 Mini Lesson

Before you describe any small detail, write one sentence that names the single biggest pattern in the data. This is called the overview, and it is the most important sentence in any Task 1 report.

Overall, ___ (category) was the most popular ___ (thing).

Do not list every number in your overview — just name the one biggest pattern you can see.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (favourite school subjects) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • Overall, maths was the most popular subject among the students surveyed.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gap using the data in the table below (not the model sentence above):

Overall, ___ (the mode with the highest percentage) was the most popular way for commuters to travel.

Now write your own overview sentence in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • My sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I started with "Overall" or "In general".
  • I named the bus as the most popular mode of transport.
  • I wrote only ONE sentence, not a full description of every number.

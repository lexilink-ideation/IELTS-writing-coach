C1 Task 2

Unit 3: Abstract Topic Discussion

Skill focus: Task 2 — Abstract Reasoning

1 Mini Lesson

At C1 level, the strongest answers move beyond surface-level claims to discuss an abstract idea — but they always ground that idea in a concrete, specific example so the argument stays convincing rather than vague.

At a fundamental level, ___ (abstract idea). This can be seen concretely in ___ (specific example). Consequently, ___ (broader implication).

Without the concrete example, an abstract claim can sound impressive but empty. The example is what proves you can actually support the idea.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (AI and homework) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • At a fundamental level, the rise of AI tools raises questions about what genuine learning actually means. This can be seen concretely in students using AI to generate essays without engaging with the underlying material. Consequently, educators may need to redesign homework tasks to assess reasoning processes rather than final written products.

3 Guided Practice

At a fundamental level, automation ___ (abstract claim about work). This can be seen concretely in ___ (specific example). Consequently, ___ (broader implication).

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I opened with an abstract framing phrase (e.g. "at a fundamental level").
  • I grounded the idea in a concrete example (e.g. "this can be seen concretely in").
  • I closed with an implication phrase (e.g. "consequently").
  • My response is about automation and the future of work.
  • I wrote at least 70 words.

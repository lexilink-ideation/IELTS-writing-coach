A1 Task 1

Unit 8: Naming the Highest Category

Skill focus: Task 1 — Highest Item (Simple)

1 Mini Lesson

You can name the biggest number in a table with one simple sentence. Use "the most popular is" and then give its number.

The most popular ___ (category type) is ___ (option), with ___ (number).

This one sentence tells the reader the single most important fact in the table.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (favourite pets) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • The most popular pet is dogs, with 42 votes.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the data in the table below (not the model sentence above):

The most popular way to travel to work is ___ (option), with ___ (number) people.

Now write your full sentence yourself in the writing box below.

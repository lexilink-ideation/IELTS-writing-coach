A2 Task 1

Unit 3: Describing a Trend Over Three Points

Skill focus: Task 1 — Trend Description with Linking Words

1 Mini Lesson

When data has three time points, don't repeat the same sentence pattern three times. Link the points together with "then" and "after that" so your writing flows.

In ___ (year 1), ___% ___ (fact). Then, in ___ (year 2), this ___ (rose/fell) to ___%. After that, by ___ (year 3), it ___ (reached/dropped to) ___%.

"Then" links the first two points; "after that" links the third.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (recycling rates) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • In 2015, 20% of people recycled regularly. Then, in 2017, this rose to 35%. After that, by 2019, it reached 50%.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the data in the table below (not the model sentence above):

In 2018, ___% of commuters used public transport. Then, in 2020, this ___ (rose/fell) to ___%. After that, by 2022, it ___ (reached/dropped to) ___%.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My first sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I included all three years: 2018, 2020, and 2022.
  • I used "then" to link the second point.
  • I used "after that" to link the third point.
  • I used a trend word (rose, fell, increased, decreased).
  • My description is about public transport.

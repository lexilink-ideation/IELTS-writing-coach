B1 Task 1

Unit 10: Table Report: Intro Plus Overview

Skill focus: Task 1 — Table Report Structure

1 Mini Lesson

A full table report opens with two sentences: an introduction that paraphrases the title, then an overview that names the single biggest pattern. In your overview, group small figures together instead of listing every number, and single out any figure that is unusually high.

The table shows ___ (topic). Overall, ___ (standout category) stands out with ___%, while the remaining categories — ___, ___, and ___ — each account for less than ___%.

Grouping small numbers together, rather than describing each one in turn, is the real strategy examiners reward in table reports.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (how households heat their homes) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • The table shows how households in four countries heat their homes. Overall, gas heating is used far more than any other method, at 78%, while the remaining methods — electric, oil, and wood heating — each account for less than 10%.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the data in the table below (not the model paragraph above):

The table shows ___ (what the table is about). Overall, the ___ (region) stands out with ___%, while the remaining regions — ___, ___, and ___ — each account for less than ___%.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My first sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I opened with an introduction sentence using "the table shows".
  • My overview sentence starts with "Overall" or "In general".
  • I singled out the East Region as the standout figure.
  • I grouped the West, South, and North Regions together instead of listing each separately.
  • I wrote at least 28 words.

C1 Task 2

Unit 1: Argument with Counter-Point

Skill focus: Task 2 — Nuanced Argumentation

1 Mini Lesson

At C1 level, examiners reward answers that acknowledge an opposing view before rebutting it, rather than ignoring the other side of the argument entirely.

It is true that / While ___ (opposing view). However, ___ (your rebuttal). As a result, ___ (consequence).

This structure shows critical thinking: you show you understand the counter-argument, then explain why your position still holds.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (the environment) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • It is sometimes argued that protecting the environment slows down economic growth. However, this concern overlooks the long-term costs of inaction, such as rising healthcare expenses caused by pollution. As a result, investing in clean technology often creates new industries and jobs, meaning environmental protection and economic growth are not necessarily in conflict.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I opened by acknowledging the opposing view (e.g. "it is true that").
  • I used a contrast connector such as "however" before my rebuttal.
  • I used a cause-effect phrase such as "as a result" for the consequence.
  • My paragraph is about automation and workers.
  • I wrote at least 60 words.

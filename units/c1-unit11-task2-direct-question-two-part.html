C1 Task 2

Unit 11: Two Questions: Reasons and Opinion

Skill focus: Task 2 — Direct Question (Two-Part)

1 Mini Lesson

A real Direct Question Essay Type 3 prompt asks two separate questions: first the reasons behind a trend, then your opinion on it. Answer each question fully and keep them clearly separate — do not let your reasons and your opinion blur into one vague statement.

Firstly, regarding the reasons behind ___ (trend), this is largely due to ___ (reason). Secondly, in terms of my opinion on this trend, I would argue that ___ (stance), since ___ (justification).

Hedging language such as "I would argue that" and "at best" signals a nuanced, qualified opinion rather than a blunt, oversimplified one.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (students using AI tools instead of textbooks) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • Firstly, regarding the reasons behind students' growing preference for AI tools over textbooks, this is largely due to the instant, tailored explanations these tools can provide, something a static textbook simply cannot replicate. Secondly, in terms of my opinion on this trend, I would argue that it is a mixed development at best, since while it undoubtedly saves time, it may also weaken the research and reading-comprehension skills that traditional textbook study helps to build.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

Firstly, regarding the reasons behind the growing preference for video adaptations over books, this is largely due to ___. Secondly, in terms of my opinion on this trend, I would argue that ___, since ___.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I answered the "reasons" question first, signposted with "firstly".
  • I answered the "opinion" question separately, signposted with "secondly".
  • I justified my opinion with "since" or a similar connector.
  • I used at least one hedging phrase (e.g. "I would argue that", "at best").
  • My response is about books and videos.
  • I wrote at least 60 words.

Unit 5: Cause and Effect Structure

Skill focus: Task 2 — Cause-Effect Writing

1 Mini Lesson

A cause-effect paragraph states something that is happening, shows its effect with "as a result", and then explains the wider implication with "this means that".

___ (cause). As a result, ___ (effect). This means that ___ (implication).

"As a result" and "this means that" build a clear chain of reasoning that examiners look for in Task Response.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (public transport) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • More cities have introduced electric buses in recent years. As a result, air pollution from public transport has decreased significantly. This means that city residents now breathe cleaner air, especially in areas near busy bus routes.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

Many people replace their smartphones and other devices very often. As a result, ___ (effect on the environment). This means that ___ (implication).

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My first sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "as a result" to introduce an effect.
  • I used "this means that" to state a wider implication.
  • My paragraph is about technology and the environment.
  • I wrote at least 35 words.

B2 Task 2

Unit 1: Advantages and Disadvantages

Skill focus: Task 2 — Balanced Argument

1 Mini Lesson

"Advantages and disadvantages" questions ask you to look at both sides before giving your view. A strong structure is one short paragraph per side, plus a brief closing opinion.

On the one hand, ___ (advantage). On the other hand, ___ (disadvantage). Overall, I believe ___.

Keep each side balanced — don't spend three sentences on one side and one word on the other.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (online classes) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • Online classes have both advantages and disadvantages. On the one hand, they allow students to study flexibly from anywhere, which saves time and travel costs. On the other hand, students may feel isolated because they have less face-to-face contact with teachers and classmates. Overall, I believe the convenience of online classes outweighs the drawbacks for most learners.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

Studying abroad has both advantages and disadvantages. On the one hand, ___. On the other hand, ___. Overall, ___.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I used "on the one hand" / "on the other hand" to organise both sides.
  • I mentioned at least one advantage.
  • I mentioned at least one disadvantage.
  • I closed with "overall" or a similar phrase and my own opinion.
  • My paragraph is about studying abroad.
  • I wrote at least 45 words.

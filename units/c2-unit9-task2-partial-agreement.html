C2 Task 2

Unit 9: Partial Agreement

Skill focus: Task 2 — Nuanced Opinion Essay

1 Mini Lesson

Partial agreement is a legitimate, and often stronger, IELTS strategy than simple agreement or disagreement. Agree that a proposed solution has genuine merit, but argue that a different solution would be more effective overall.

It is true that ___ (proposed solution) can help to ___ (benefit). However, a more effective approach would be to ___ (alternative solution), since this would ___ (stronger reason).

Partial agreement shows the examiner you can evaluate an idea critically rather than simply accepting or rejecting it outright.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (public transport) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • Some argue that reducing bus fares is the best way to encourage people to give up private cars. It is true that lower fares can help to make public transport more accessible to price-sensitive commuters. However, a more effective approach would be to invest in dedicated bus lanes and higher service frequency, since this would address the reliability concerns that deter most regular car users far more directly than price alone.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

Some argue that banning AI tools is the best way to preserve the educational value of homework. It is true that ___. However, a more effective approach would be to ___, since this would ___.

Now write your full response yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I conceded genuine merit in the proposed solution using "it is true that".
  • I introduced my alternative with "however, a more effective approach would be".
  • I justified my alternative using "since this would" (or a similar reason connector).
  • My response is about AI and homework.
  • I wrote at least 85 words.

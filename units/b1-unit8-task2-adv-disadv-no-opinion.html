B1 Task 2

Unit 8: Advantages and Disadvantages, No Opinion

Skill focus: Task 2 — Pure Advantages & Disadvantages (Type 1)

1 Mini Lesson

Not every Task 2 essay asks for your opinion. The Advantages & Disadvantages essay (Type 1) asks you to present one clear advantage and one clear disadvantage in a balanced way — with zero personal opinion. Do not write "I think" or "in my opinion" anywhere in this essay type.

One advantage of ___ is that ___. However, one disadvantage is that ___.

Students often lose marks here by adding their own opinion out of habit — this essay type specifically forbids it.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (online classes) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • One advantage of online classes is that students can learn from home without spending time or money on travel. However, one disadvantage is that some students find it difficult to stay motivated without a teacher present in the room.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

One advantage of automation in the workplace is that ___. However, one disadvantage is that ___.

Now write your full paragraph yourself in the writing box below — remember, no personal opinion.

4 Writing Box

Prompt: What are the advantages and disadvantages of automation (machines and AI doing jobs) in the workplace?

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I named ONE clear advantage using "one advantage is that".
  • I used "However" or "on the other hand" to move to the disadvantage.
  • I named ONE clear disadvantage using "one disadvantage is that".
  • I did NOT use "I think" or "in my opinion" — this essay type has no personal opinion.
  • My paragraph is about automation and jobs.
  • I wrote at least 35 words.

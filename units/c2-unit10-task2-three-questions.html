C2 Task 2

Unit 10: Three Questions in One Essay

Skill focus: Task 2 — Direct Question (Three-Part)

1 Mini Lesson

The most demanding Direct Question prompt asks three separate questions in one task. Each must be answered fully and separately — never blended together — and clearly signposted with sequencing language.

Firstly, regarding why ___, this is largely because ___. Secondly, in terms of the problems this creates, ___. Finally, to address this, ___ (solution) could help, since this would ___.

Three clearly signposted answers, each with its own reasoning, is what separates a Band 8-9 response from one that blurs all three questions into a single vague paragraph.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (AI and homework) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • Firstly, regarding why AI tools have become so widespread in homework, this is largely because they offer instant, personalised assistance that traditional resources cannot match. Secondly, in terms of the problems this creates, over-reliance on AI risks eroding students' independent problem-solving skills, since they may submit generated answers without genuinely engaging with the underlying material. Finally, to address this, schools could redesign assignments to require students to critique or extend AI-generated work rather than simply reproduce it, since this would preserve the critical-thinking benefits homework is meant to provide.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

Firstly, regarding why many older people struggle with modern technology, this is largely because ___. Secondly, in terms of the problems this creates, ___. Finally, to address this, ___, since this would ___.

Now write your full three-part response yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I answered the "why" question first, signposted with "firstly".
  • I answered the "problems" question separately, signposted with "secondly".
  • I answered the "solution" question separately, signposted with "finally".
  • I justified at least one answer with "because" or "since".
  • My response is about older people and technology.
  • I wrote at least 90 words.

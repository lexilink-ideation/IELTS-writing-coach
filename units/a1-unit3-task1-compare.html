A1 Task 1

Unit 3: Comparing Two Numbers

Skill focus: Task 1 — Comparison

1 Mini Lesson

IELTS Task 1 often asks you to compare two pieces of data — for example, a number from one year and a number from another year. Useful comparison language:

more than  |  higher than  |  increased from X to Y

"Increased from X to Y" is the strongest pattern — it shows the starting number and the ending number clearly.

2 Model Sentences

These examples use a different topic (public transport) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • The number increased from 20 to 55.
  • 55 is higher than 20.
  • More people used public transport in 2023 than in 2019.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • My sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I included both numbers, 40 and 70.
  • I used a comparison word (more than / higher than / increased).
  • If I used "increased", I wrote "from 40 to 70".

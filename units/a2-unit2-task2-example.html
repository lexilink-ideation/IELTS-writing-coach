A2 Task 2

Unit 2: Opinion + Reason + Example

Skill focus: Task 2 — Idea Development

1 Mini Lesson

You can make your reason stronger and more convincing by adding a specific example. Use the phrase:

for example

An example turns a general reason into a detailed, believable point — exactly what IELTS examiners look for in Task Response.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (public transport) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • I think public transport is important because it reduces traffic in cities. For example, cities with good bus networks tend to have less congestion during rush hour.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model sentences above):

___ (I think / I believe) online classes are / are not effective because ___. For example, ___.

Now write your full answer yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My first sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "I think" / "I believe" / "In my opinion".
  • I used "because" to give a reason.
  • I used "for example" to support my reason.
  • My answer is about online classes.

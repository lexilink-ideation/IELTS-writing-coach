A1 Task 2

Unit 13: Yes or No, One Reason

Skill focus: Task 2 — Direct Question Basics

1 Mini Lesson

Some IELTS questions simply ask Yes or No. This is different from a full opinion essay — you do not need "In my opinion". Instead, answer directly with "Yes, I think..." or "No, I don't think...", then add one reason with "because".

Yes, I think ___ / No, I don't think ___ because ___.

A Direct Question answer is short and direct — one clear Yes/No sentence, not a full paragraph.

2 Model Sentences

These examples use a different topic (online classes) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • Yes, I think online classes are good because students can learn from home.
  • No, I don't think online classes are good because students miss their friends.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model sentences above):

___ (Yes, I think / No, I don't think) more people should use public transport because ___.

Now write your own Yes/No answer in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • My sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I answered directly with "Yes, I think" or "No, I don't think" — not "In my opinion".
  • I used "because" to give one reason.
  • My sentence is about public transport.

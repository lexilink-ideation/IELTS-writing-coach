C1 Task 1

Unit 12: A Man-Made Process, Passive Voice

Skill focus: Task 1 — Process (Man-Made Type)

1 Mini Lesson

Unlike a natural cycle, which has no fixed start or end and repeats indefinitely, a man-made process moves in one direction toward a single final product. Because the machinery or system — not any one person — carries out each stage, man-made processes should be described almost entirely in the passive voice ("is collected", "are sorted").

The process begins with ___ (stage 1, passive). Subsequently, ___ (stage 2, passive). This is followed by ___ (stage 3, passive). At the penultimate stage, ___ (stage 4, passive). The process culminates in ___ (final stage, passive).

If your process ends at a fixed final product rather than looping back to stage 1, it is a man-made process — keep every stage in the passive voice throughout.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (assembling a smartphone on an automated production line) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new process below.

  • The process begins with individual components being placed onto the production line. Subsequently, the circuit boards are inserted and connected by robotic arms. This is followed by a testing stage, during which each device is checked for faults. At the penultimate stage, functioning units are packaged into their retail boxes. The process culminates in the finished smartphones being shipped to distribution centres worldwide.

3 Guided Practice

The process begins with ___ (collection, passive). Subsequently, ___ (sorting, passive). This is followed by ___ (washing, passive). At the penultimate stage, ___ (shredding, passive). The process culminates in ___ (melting and reforming, passive).

4 Writing Box

Prompt: The diagram below shows the stages involved in recycling plastic bottles, from collection to the production of new plastic pellets. Summarise the process by describing each stage in order, using appropriate sequencing language and the passive voice throughout.

Stages: Recycling Plastic Bottles

StageWhat Happens
1. CollectionUsed plastic bottles are collected from recycling bins and depots
2. SortingThe bottles are sorted by colour and plastic type
3. WashingThe sorted bottles are washed to remove labels, glue, and contaminants
4. ShreddingThe clean bottles are shredded into small plastic flakes
5. Melting & ReformingThe flakes are melted and reformed into new plastic pellets

Look at the stages. Now describe the whole process in order, keeping every stage in the passive voice — don't copy the lesson example.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I opened with "the process begins with".
  • I used "subsequently" for the next stage.
  • I used "this is followed by" or "at the penultimate stage" for a later stage.
  • I closed with "the process culminates in" or "finally".
  • I used the passive voice for every stage (e.g. "are collected", "are sorted", "are washed").
  • My description mentions plastic bottles or recycling.
  • I wrote at least 65 words.

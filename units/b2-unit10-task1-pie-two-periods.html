B2 Task 1

Unit 10: Pie Charts: Two Time Periods

Skill focus: Task 1 — Pie Chart Comparison

1 Mini Lesson

When you are given two pie charts from different years, do not describe each chart separately. Instead, focus on the key changes between them — which categories rose, which fell, and by how much.

In ___ (year 1), ___ (category) accounted for the largest share, at ___%. By ___ (year 2), however, this had fallen to ___%, while ___ (category) rose sharply from ___% to ___%.

Naming only the categories that changed the most — and ignoring the ones that stayed similar — keeps your comparison focused instead of a flat re-description of both charts.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (how students access course materials) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • In 2005, printed textbooks accounted for the largest share of course materials, at 70%. By 2020, however, this had fallen sharply to 20%, while online resources rose dramatically from 15% to 65% over the same period.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the data in the table below (not the model paragraph above):

In 2000, ___ (source) accounted for the largest share of electricity generation, at ___%. By 2020, however, this had fallen to ___%, while ___ (source) rose from ___% to ___%.

Now write your full comparison yourself in the writing box below.

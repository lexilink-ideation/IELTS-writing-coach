B2 Task 1

Unit 4: Comparing Multiple Data Sets

Skill focus: Task 1 — Varied Comparative Vocabulary

1 Mini Lesson

When you describe change across three time points, repeating "more than" every time becomes monotonous and limits your Lexical Resource score. Instead, vary your comparative language: use words like "considerably", "substantially", and "slightly" to show different sizes of change.

In ___ (year 1), ___ (relationship, varied language). By ___ (year 2), ___ (change, varied language). By ___ (year 3), ___ (further change, varied language). Overall, ___ (synthesis).

Choosing precise words instead of repeating "more than" shows the examiner a wider range of vocabulary.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (car vs. bicycle commuting) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • In 2016, car commuting was considerably more common than cycling, accounting for 70% of journeys compared to just 15% by bicycle. By 2020, the gap had narrowed substantially, with cycling rising to 35%. By 2024, cycling had grown only slightly further, reaching 40%. Overall, the data suggests cycling gained ground steadily but has recently begun to plateau.

3 Guided Practice

In 2018, online classes were ___ (varied language) less common than in-person classes. By 2021, ___ (varied language, avoid "more than"). By 2024, ___ (varied language). Overall, ___ (synthesis).

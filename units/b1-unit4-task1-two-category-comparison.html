B1 Task 1

Unit 4: Comparing Two Categories Over Time

Skill focus: Task 1 — Two-Category Comparison

1 Mini Lesson

When a table shows two categories across two years, describe the first year, then the second year, and link them with a contrast connector so the reader sees how the relationship between the categories changed.

In ___ (year 1), ___% ___ (category A), compared to ___% who ___ (category B). By ___ (year 2), this had changed: ___% ___ (category A), whereas ___% ___ (category B).

"Whereas" and "compared to" link the two categories together in one flowing description.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (phone calls vs. text messages) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • In 2010, phone calls made up 65% of communication methods, compared to 35% for text messages. By 2020, this had reversed, with text messages rising to 70% whereas phone calls fell to 30%.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the data in the table below (not the model sentence above):

In 2015, ___% of students preferred books, compared to ___% who preferred videos. By 2023, this had changed: ___% preferred books, whereas ___% preferred videos.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I used a contrast connector such as "whereas" or "compared to".

A2 Task 1

Unit 11: What Happens Next? (Two-Step Process)

Skill focus: Task 1 — Process Basics (Two Steps)

1 Mini Lesson

When a process has two stages, describe both of them in order. Use "First," for the first stage, then "Then, after that," to introduce the second stage clearly.

First, ___ (stage 1). Then, after that, ___ (stage 2).

"Then, after that" tells the reader a new stage is starting, without needing "next" or "finally" yet.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (opening a bank account) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new process below.

  • First, the customer fills out an application form. Then, after that, the bank issues a debit card.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the steps in the table below (not the model sentence above):

First, the student ___ (step 1). Then, after that, the embassy ___ (step 2).

Now write both sentences yourself in the writing box below.

4 Writing Box

Prompt: The table below shows the first two steps of applying for a study visa. Describe both steps in order using "First," and "Then, after that,".

Steps: Applying for a Study Visa

StepWhat Happens
1The student submits the application form and documents
2The embassy reviews the application and schedules an interview

Look at both steps. Now describe both of them in order — don't copy the lesson example.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My first sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "First," for step 1.
  • I used "Then, after that," for step 2.
  • My description mentions the visa application and the embassy.

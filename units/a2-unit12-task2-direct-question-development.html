A2 Task 2

Unit 12: Positive or Negative? One Paragraph

Skill focus: Task 2 — Direct Question (Development)

1 Mini Lesson

Some Direct Question prompts ask if a trend is a "positive development" or a "negative development". Choose one side clearly in your first sentence, then give one reason with "because".

In my opinion, ___ (trend) is a positive/negative development because ___ (reason).

Pick only one side — a Direct Question answer commits to a clear position, not a balanced discussion of both.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (online classes) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • In my opinion, the growing popularity of online classes is a positive development because students can learn at times that suit them.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model sentence above):

In my opinion, older people using technology is a positive/negative development because ___.

Now write your full paragraph yourself in the writing box below.

4 Writing Box

Prompt: Some people believe that older people using technology (like smartphones and computers) is a positive development, while others believe it is negative. What is your opinion?

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • My sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I opened with "In my opinion" and a clear stance.
  • I called it a "positive development" or "negative development" — not both.
  • I gave one reason using "because".
  • My paragraph is about older people and technology.

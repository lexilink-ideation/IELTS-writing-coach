A2 Task 2

Unit 1: Opinion + Reason

Skill focus: Task 2 — Paragraph Building

1 Mini Lesson

At A2 level, a good IELTS Task 2 answer is usually two sentences: an opinion sentence with "because", followed by one extra sentence that supports it.

Opinion + because + reason. Then one more sentence to support the reason.

The second sentence should add a small extra detail — it does not need a new linking word, just a related idea.

2 Model Sentences

These examples use a different topic (the environment) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • I think protecting the environment is important because pollution harms our health. Dirty air can make people sick, especially children.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model sentences above):

___ (I think / I believe) students should / should not use AI for homework because ___. ___ (add one more sentence to support your reason).

Now write your two sentences yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My first sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • Every sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "I think" / "I believe" / "In my opinion".
  • I used "because" to give a reason.
  • I wrote a second sentence that supports my reason.
  • My answer is about AI and homework.

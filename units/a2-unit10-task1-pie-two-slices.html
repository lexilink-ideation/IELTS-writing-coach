A2 Task 1

Unit 10: A Pie Chart with Two Slices

Skill focus: Task 1 — Pie Chart Two-Part

1 Mini Lesson

You already know "accounts for". Now add a second pie-chart phrase, "represents", and describe two slices in one sentence.

___ (category 1) accounts for ___% of ___ (total), while ___ (category 2) represents ___%.

Using two different phrases for two slices shows a wider vocabulary range than repeating "accounts for" twice.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (journeys to work) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • Buses accounts for 40% of journeys to work, while trains represents 25%.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the data in the pie chart below (not the model sentence above):

___ (material) accounts for ___% of recycled waste, while ___ (material) represents ___%.

Now write your full sentence yourself in the writing box below.

4 Writing Box

Prompt: The pie chart below shows the materials collected for recycling in a city. Describe the two largest slices, using "accounts for" and "represents".

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • My sentence starts with a capital letter.
  • My sentence ends with a full stop.
  • I used "accounts for" for the first slice.
  • I used "represents" for the second slice.
  • I named both "plastic" and "paper".
  • I included both percentages, 35% and 25%.

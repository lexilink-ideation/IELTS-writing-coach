B2 Task 1

Unit 6: Pie Chart Proportions

Skill focus: Task 1 — Proportion Description

1 Mini Lesson

Pie charts reward proportion language rather than raw percentages alone. Use phrases like "a quarter of", "nearly half", and "the majority" to describe the size of each slice in natural English.

___ (proportion phrase) of ___ (total) is spent on ___ (category 1). ___ (proportion phrase) is spent on ___ (category 2). ___ (proportion phrase) is spent on ___ (category 3). Overall, ___ (synthesis).

Pairing a proportion phrase with its exact percentage — e.g. "nearly half, at 45%" — gives you both natural language and precise data in the same sentence.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (how children spend their screen time) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • Nearly half of children's screen time is spent playing games, accounting for 50% of the total. A significant proportion, almost a third, is spent watching videos, at 30%. The remaining fifth, comprising just 20%, goes toward social media. Overall, gaming clearly dominates how children spend their screen time.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the data in the table below (not the model paragraph above):

___ (proportion phrase) of study time is spent on homework, at ___%. ___ (proportion phrase) is spent on reading, at ___%. ___ (proportion phrase) is spent on group projects, at ___%, and the smallest share, ___%, is spent on online videos. Overall, ___ (synthesis).

Now write your full description yourself in the writing box below.

Unit 6: Comprehensive Task 1 Report

Skill focus: Task 1 — Full Task 1 Report

1 Mini Lesson

A complete multi-year report combines everything you have practised so far: an overview of the overall pattern, a trend description that moves through the data point by point, and an explicit comparison between categories — all closed with a final synthesis statement.

Overall, ___ (single biggest pattern across the whole period). In ___ (year 1), ___ (starting comparison). By ___ (year 2), ___ (change). This trend continued/reversed by ___ (year 3), ___ (further change). Taken together, ___ (synthesis).

Without weaving the overview, trend, and comparison into one flowing report, a Task 1 answer reads as disconnected observations rather than genuine analysis.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (attitudes toward electric vehicles) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new data below.

  • Overall, enthusiasm for electric vehicles grew steadily among surveyed drivers between 2018 and 2024, though the pace of change varied across the period. In 2018, only 12% of respondents expressed interest in an electric vehicle, considerably fewer than the 68% who preferred petrol cars. By 2020, interest had risen modestly to 22%, while petrol car preference slipped to 58%. This upward trend accelerated sharply thereafter, and by 2024 electric vehicles had overtaken petrol cars in popularity for the first time, favoured by 61% of respondents compared to just 30% for petrol. Taken together, the data reveals a decisive and accelerating shift in consumer preference toward electric vehicles.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the data in the table below (not the model paragraph above):

Overall, preference for online classes ___ (overview pattern) between 2020 and 2023. In 2020, only ___% of students preferred online classes, compared to ___% who preferred in-person. By 2023, this had ___ (change language), with ___% now preferring online and ___% preferring in-person. Taken together, ___ (synthesis).

Now write your full report yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I opened with an overview phrase such as "overall".
  • I reported the 2020 figures (15% and 85%).
  • I reported the 2023 figures (70% and 30%).
  • I used trend language such as "rose", "increased", "declined", or "fell".
  • I used a contrast connector such as "whereas", "while", or "compared to".
  • I closed with a synthesis phrase such as "taken together".
  • I wrote at least 100 words.

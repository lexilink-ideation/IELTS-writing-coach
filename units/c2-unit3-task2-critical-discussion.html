C2 Task 2

Unit 3: Philosophical IELTS Topic Discussion

Skill focus: Task 2 — Critical Discussion

1 Mini Lesson

At C2 level, the strongest responses treat a claim critically rather than simply agreeing or disagreeing: present the claim, question it with a counter-perspective, then synthesise both into a deeper, nuanced conclusion.

It is sometimes claimed that ___ (claim). Critics might contend that ___ (counter-perspective). On a deeper level, ___ (synthesis). Ultimately, ___ (nuanced conclusion).

The goal is not to "win" the argument but to show you can hold two perspectives in tension and reach a genuinely considered view.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (ageing populations and technology) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • It is often assumed that technological literacy is simply a skill older people lack, yet this framing arguably obscures a more fundamental question about how societies choose to design technology in the first place. Critics might contend that placing the burden of adaptation solely on older users ignores design choices that could make technology more universally accessible. Ultimately, the debate over ageing populations and technology reveals broader tensions between individual adaptability and collective responsibility for inclusive design.

5 Self-Review Checklist

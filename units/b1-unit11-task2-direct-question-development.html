B1 Task 2

Unit 11: Positive or Negative Development (Full)

Skill focus: Task 2 — Direct Question (Development, Full)

1 Mini Lesson

A "positive or negative development" prompt asks you to take a clear side and support it with a reason and a result. This is a real Direct Question essay type (Type 2) — your job is to commit to one side, not describe both equally.

In my opinion, ___ (trend) is a positive/negative development. This is mainly because ___ (reason). As a result, ___ (result).

State your stance in the very first sentence — do not leave the reader guessing which side you support.

2 Model Sentences

This example uses a different topic (electric buses in cities) so you can see the pattern — then apply it to the new topic below.

  • In my opinion, the increasing use of electric buses in cities is a positive development. This is mainly because they produce far fewer emissions than traditional diesel buses, which helps to improve air quality. As a result, cities that invest in electric buses are likely to become healthier and more sustainable places to live.

3 Guided Practice

Fill in the gaps using the topic below (not the model paragraph above):

In my opinion, the growing amount of time children spend on screens is a positive/negative development. This is mainly because ___. As a result, ___.

Now write your full paragraph yourself in the writing box below.

5 Self-Review Checklist

  • I opened with "In my opinion" and a clear stance.
  • I called it a "positive development" or "negative development" — not both.
  • I gave a reason using "this is mainly because".
  • I gave a result using "as a result" or "this means that".
  • My paragraph is about children and screen time.
  • I wrote at least 35 words.
